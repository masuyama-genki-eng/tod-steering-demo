Local TOD control in symbolic arrangement with SAE directions

Each example steers the temporal onset density (TOD) of one instrument in one two-bar section while the arrangement model generates. Compare the selected SAE direction with a matched random direction at the same strengths λ, against generation without intervention.

  1. Pick a strength in the grid. Switching keeps the playback position, so you can compare conditions at the same moment.
  2. Watch the shaded bars in the piano roll. Only that section of the target track receives the intervention.
  3. Use “Target track only” when the instrument is hard to hear in the full arrangement.
